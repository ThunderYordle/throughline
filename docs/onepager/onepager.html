Checkable

Typed items the review can verify

Objective, concede, lane, window, spike and side items are checked from player positions, objective spawns, plates, fights and gold, not from a VOD scrub.

Honest

Does following the plan win?

Plans built only from earlier games, checked on every game after, and win rate compared by adherence at equal gold. The result is reported either way.
