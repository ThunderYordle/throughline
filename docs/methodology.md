# Methodology

## 1. The problem and the approach

A draft is a set of promises.
A coach drafts an early-game comp because it should take the first dragons, or a scaling comp because it should trade objectives and survive.
Those promises live in the coach's head, and nothing writes them down in a form the team can check.

Throughline closes the loop in two steps.
The Plan turns a draft into 4 to 8 typed, checkable items, each backed by counted data from earlier pro games.
The Review checks each item against the game that was played, and detects the macro moments the plan did not cover.
No text is generated by a language model.
Every line is a template filled from counted data, with its sample size.

## 2. Data

### Events used

The input is the hackathon's GRID starter data, one zstd-compressed JSON Lines feed per series.
Throughline reads champion picks, kills and assists, epic monster kills and spawns, tower, inhibitor and nexus destructions, turret plates, level-ups, item purchases and undos, per-player gold and per-player positions.

### Census

The census of one series (`2964589`, see `docs/data/events.md`) found 74 event types.
Positions arrive with 10,078 observed gaps between changes: median 3 s, 90th percentile 8 s, maximum 62 s.

### Dataset

1,738 games parse, across 20 tournaments (LCK, LEC, LTA, LCS), patches 15.1 to 16.17, from 2025-01-15 to 2026-10-13.
One game fails and is skipped (`3ac8f0c2-a0fc-4995-9ce1-8f4d31815c63`: blue has 6 players with a champion).
The derived `participant_features` table has 17,380 participant rows.
Level 6 is found for 99.90% of them (median 6:51, 411 s), and the first legendary item for 99.95% (median 12:36, 756 s).

Objective spawn times come from the feed, not from a table, because they changed between seasons.
Median first spawns, Season 15 against Season 16:

| Objective | Season 15 | Season 16 |
|---|---|---|
| Dragon | 4:54 | 4:54 |
| Void grubs | 7:53 | 7:53 |
| Rift Herald | 14:57 | 14:57 |
| Baron | 24:40 | 19:40 |

### What the feed cannot give

- **Positions every few seconds.** `GameIndex.position` interpolates between two observations at most `POSITION_MAX_GAP` = 20 s apart, and otherwise accepts an observation within `POSITION_TOLERANCE` = 5 s. Beyond that the position is unknown, and callers must handle "unknown". No frame is assumed every second.
- **No wards.** There are no ward or vision checks anywhere. Objective items check who grouped first instead.
- **No roles.** The `roles` field is always empty. Roles are inferred per team: the support from time near the World Atlas support markers, the jungler from scuttle and off-lane time, the laners from lane time.
- **A clock a few seconds behind the broadcast.** Known spawns (dragon at 4:54) show the feed's clock trails the broadcast timer by a few seconds. Every time in the app is the feed's time.

## 3. Knowledge base

The knowledge base is built per patch window: the game's patch and the two before it (`PATCH_WINDOW` = 3 patches), widened to the season so far and then to the whole dataset when samples are thin (`MIN_SCOPE_GAMES` = 200).
Only games played before the planned game are used.

- **Tempo curves.** Per champion and role, the team's gold difference at 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30 minutes against the role's league average.
- **Shrinkage.** Every curve value is shrunk toward zero by `value * n / (n + 20)` (`PRIOR_GAMES` = 20), so a champion with 4 games barely moves a curve.
- **Matchups.** Gold and CS difference at 10 and 14 minutes for a champion pair in a lane, with at least `MIN_MATCHUP_GAMES` = 5 games. Otherwise each champion's own lane curve is used.
- **Spikes.** The median time each champion reaches level 6 and completes its first and second legendary item, with the gold-difference change in the 3 minutes after. At least `MIN_SPIKE_GAMES` = 10 games.
- **Objective tendencies.** Per champion, how much the team's first-dragon, grubs, herald and baron rates differ from the league, shrunk the same way.
- **Spawn times.** Median first spawn per objective, per season, with at least `MIN_SPAWN_GAMES` = 10 games.

## 4. The plan

The two team curves are summed per team, with a matchup term for each lane that has one.
Their difference is the draft tempo.
A window is a stretch where the draft tempo is beyond `WINDOW_GOLD`, labelled "our window" or "their window".
A lane has priority when its 10-minute edge is beyond `LANE_GOLD`.

| Item type | How it is built | Guard |
|---|---|---|
| `objective` | Lane priority near the pit, draft tempo at spawn, objective tendencies, the season's spawn time | Signal at least `OBJECTIVE_SIGNAL`, games at least `MIN_ITEM_GAMES` |
| `concede` | The opponent's priority near the pit and their window | Same signal guard |
| `lane` | A lane matchup, until `LANE_UNTIL` | Games at least `MIN_ITEM_GAMES` |
| `window` | A stretch of draft tempo beyond `WINDOW_GOLD` | Games at least `MIN_ITEM_GAMES` |
| `spike` | A level-6 or item spike with a gain beyond `SPIKE_GOLD` | Games at least `MIN_ITEM_GAMES` |
| `side` | Lane priorities, jungler's side until `SIDE_UNTIL`, weighted by `SIDE_WEIGHT` | Games at least `MIN_ITEM_GAMES` |

Items are ranked by signal strength and games behind them, discounted until `CONFIDENT_GAMES` games, and the top `MAX_ITEMS` make the plan.
An item with fewer games than its guard is never generated.

Constants from `backend/throughline/plan/constants.py`:

| Constant | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `WINDOW_GOLD` | 300 | Draft tempo beyond this is a window |
| `LANE_GOLD` | 150 | Lane edge at 10:00 beyond this is priority |
| `SPIKE_GOLD` | 250 | Team gold gained in the 3 minutes after a spike |
| `TENDENCY_UNIT` | 0.10 | Objective-rate edge that counts as one unit of signal |
| `OBJECTIVE_SIGNAL` | 1.0 | Signal needed to contest or concede an objective |
| `MIN_ITEM_GAMES` | 10 | Games needed behind every piece of evidence |
| `CONFIDENT_GAMES` | 50 | Games at which an item's score is no longer discounted |
| `MAX_ITEMS` | 8 | Largest plan |
| `LANE_UNTIL` | 480 s | End of a lane item's range |
| `SIDE_UNTIL` | 360 s | End of a side item's range |
| `SIDE_WEIGHT` | 0.8 | Weight of a side item's signal |
| `OBJECTIVE_LEAD` | 20 s | Be set up this long before the spawn |
| `OBJECTIVE_MIN_PLAYERS` | 3 | Players needed in the setup |

## 5. The review

### Plan checkers

Each verdict is followed, partly, not followed or not applicable, and states the numbers it read.

- **`objective`**: at `OBJECTIVE_LEAD` seconds before spawn, at least `OBJECTIVE_MIN_PLAYERS` players near the pit, grouped there first, and the objective taken. All three give followed. Some of them give partly. Otherwise not followed.
- **`concede`**: at spawn, at most `CONCEDE_MAX_NEAR` = 1 of our players near the pit, and what we took in the `CONCEDE_TRADE_SPAN` = 60 s around it.
- **`lane`**: plates, structures and gold difference in the lane at the end of the item's range.
- **`window`**: fights started in the range, their numbers and results.
- **`spike`**: deaths in that lane in the `SPIKE_WATCH` = 120 s after the enemy spike.
- **`side`**: the jungler's share of time per map half, sampled every `SIDE_STEP` = 5 s from `SIDE_START` = 90 s. A share of at least `SIDE_FOLLOWED` = 0.6 is followed, and at least `SIDE_PARTLY` = 0.45 is partly.

Every clip runs `CLIP_BEFORE` = 15 s before to `CLIP_AFTER` = 10 s after the deciding moment.

### Macro moments

| Moment | Rule | Constants |
|---|---|---|
| Objective setup | For every spawn, when each team first had `GROUP_SIZE` = 3 players in the pit area in the `SETUP_WINDOW` = 90 s before, sampled every `SAMPLE_STEP` = 5 s | |
| Unused advantage | At least `ADVANTAGE_KILLS` = 2 enemy deaths within `ADVANTAGE_KILL_SPAN` = 10 s, or `RECALL_MIN` = 3 recalls within `RECALL_RADIUS` = 1,500 units (from `RECALL_FROM` = 180 s), then `ADVANTAGE_WINDOW` = 60 s without an objective, structure or plate gain | |
| Cross-map trade | Objectives or structures taken by both teams within `TRADE_GAP` = 45 s on opposite halves, even when within `TRADE_EVEN` = 200 gold | |
| Caught before an objective | After `CAUGHT_FROM` = 840 s, a death with no ally within `CAUGHT_ALLY_DISTANCE` = 2,000 units in the `CAUGHT_BEFORE` = 90 s before a spawn | |
| Gold swing | Team gold difference moves at least `SWING_GOLD` = 1,500 within `SWING_SPAN` = 60 s, scanned every `SWING_STEP` = 10 s | |

Fights are grouped with `FIGHT_GAP` = 15 s and `FIGHT_JOIN_RADIUS` = 3,000 units, and a player is present within `PRESENT_RADIUS` = 2,500 units.
The impact of a moment is valued with `KILL_GOLD` = 300, `PLATE_GOLD` = 250, and per-objective and per-structure gold tables in `review/moments.py`.
When more than `MAX_MOMENTS` = 15 are found, the largest gold impact wins.

## 6. Validation

### Design

For every game on or after the holdout date, both sides' plans are built from games played before it only, and every item is checked against the game.
The holdout date is 2025-08-20, and a side needs at least `MIN_PRIOR_GAMES` = 200 prior games.
The generator's constants were tuned on the earlier split only, never on the reported one.

Following a plan is confounded with already winning, because a team that is ahead finds every item easier.
To limit that, the item-level comparison buckets followed and not-followed sides by gold difference at the item's start (edges at -1,500, -500, 500 and 1,500) and compares inside each bucket.
This limits the confounding, and it does not remove it.

Run: `throughline validate --holdout-from 2025-08-20`, 587 holdout sides.

### Win rate by adherence

Adherence is the share of items followed, in quartiles.

| Adherence | Sides | Win rate |
|---|---|---|
| 0% to 25% | 106 | 35.8% |
| 25% to 50% | 122 | 41.8% |
| 50% to 75% | 210 | 55.7% |
| 75% to 100% | 149 | 55.0% |

### Per item type

| Type | Followed win rate | Not followed win rate | Adjusted difference | Followed | Not followed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `window` | 80.3% | 31.1% | +20.4 pp | 76 | 74 |
| `lane` | 52.4% | 43.6% | +8.8 pp | 82 | 39 |
| `spike` | 46.7% | 35.7% | +7.9 pp | 137 | 14 |
| `side` | 50.0% | 47.8% | +2.2 pp | 16 | 69 |
| `concede` | 57.4% | 57.1% | -4.9 pp | 61 | 28 |
| `objective` | 100.0% | 47.4% | +41.8 pp | 1 | 247 |

### What this shows and does not show

- Win rate rises with adherence from 35.8% to about 55%, then flattens between the top two quartiles. Part of that is reverse causation: teams that are ahead follow more items.
- `window` and `lane` keep a clear edge after the gold bucketing. `spike` is positive but its not-followed group is only 14 sides.
- `side` is a small positive on few followed sides (16), and `concede` is flat to negative (-4.9 pp adjusted). Neither is evidence for the item type.
- `objective` is the honest weak spot. Only 1 side followed against 247 that did not, so the +41.8 pp figure means nothing. The rule asks for 3 or more players grouped 20 s before spawn, grouped first, and the objective taken. That marks almost all real pro setups as partly or not followed. It is the clearest candidate to recalibrate next.
- None of this is a causal claim that following the plan wins games.

## 7. Hand checks

Throughline's checkers and detectors were built test-first on small synthetic games, and the real-data runs check invariants (every game has spawns, no objective is taken before its first spawn, parsed winners and patches match `meta/games.csv`).
The planned VOD comparison, five games with every verdict and moment compared by hand, is not yet logged in `docs/BUILD_LOG.md`.
Until it is, no accuracy figure is claimed for verdicts or moments.

## 8. Limits

- Sample sizes: 1,738 games, 587 holdout sides, and small groups for `side`, `spike` and `objective` items. Every plan line shows its games count.
- Patches: the knowledge base uses the game's patch and two before it, widened when thin, so new patches lean on older data.
- Seasons: spawn times differ between Season 15 and 16 (baron 24:40 against 19:40), and are read per season.
- The feed shows no minion waves, no cooldowns, no wards, and no communication. Lane state is read from plates, structures and laner positions only.
- Positions are sampled every few seconds, and long gaps are reported as unknown.
- Role inference is a heuristic.
- The results come from one dataset, one holdout and one set of constants.
